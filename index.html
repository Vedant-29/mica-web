Hide your desktop

Mica hides your windows, Dock, wallpaper, and notifications the moment you share your screen. One keystroke, or automatic. When the call ends, everything comes back exactly as it was.

Free, open source, and made for macOS.

The moment you share your screen

Mica watches for screen sharing and recording — Zoom, Meet, Teams, QuickTime, ⌘⇧5, OBS — and hides everything the instant it starts. It reveals again when you stop.

videos/screen-share.mp4
02

Everything, or only what you choose

Hide your open windows, the Dock, your menu bar icons, your wallpaper, and your desktop icons and widgets. Nothing is closed and nothing is lost — an app you'd already hidden stays hidden.

  • Do Not DisturbSilences notifications for the duration.
  • Hide Active WindowsAll, all but the front one, or just the apps you pick.
  • Hide DockSlides it out of sight.
  • Hide Menu Bar IconsEverything left of Mica's marker.
  • Hide WallpaperA plain backdrop instead.
  • Hide Desktop Icons & WidgetsWallpaper stays; the clutter goes.
videos/windows.mp4
03

On, Auto, or Off

On hides everything now. Auto hides only while your screen is shared. Off stands down. And ⌥⌘S flips it from anywhere — a panic button that never leaves you stranded.
