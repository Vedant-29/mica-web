Hide your desktop before anyone sees it.

One keystroke — or automatically, the moment you share your screen — Mica hides your windows, Dock, wallpaper, and notifications. When the call ends, everything comes back exactly as it was.

How it works

On, Auto, or Off. That's the whole thing.

01

On

Everything you've enabled hides right now, and stays hidden until you switch back.

02

Auto

Hides the moment your screen is shared or recorded, and reveals when it stops.

03

⌥⌘S

One hotkey to flip it, wherever you are. A panic button that never strands you.
